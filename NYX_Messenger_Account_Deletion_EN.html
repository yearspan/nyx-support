Account Information Deletion Request Statement

We respect your privacy and are committed to providing transparent data management. If you wish to delete your account and associated personal data from the NYX Messenger platform, please carefully review the following instructions and conditions.

How to Delete Your Account

To initiate account deletion, please follow these steps within the app:

  1. Open the NYX Messenger app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Go to Settings > Manage Account.
  4. Scroll to the bottom, select Delete Account, and then submit your request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon successful deletion, the following data will be permanently erased:

  • Your personal profile information (e.g., username, avatar, email address).
  • Your chat records and friend list.
  • All other data associated with your account.

Please note: This action is irreversible.

Retained Data (If Applicable)

In compliance with legal or regulatory requirements, we may retain certain transaction records or security logs for a period of up to 60 days. These will be permanently deleted once the retention period expires.

Account Deletion Retention Period

  • Your deletion request will be processed within 7 business days of submission.
  • Once your data is permanently deleted, it cannot be restored.
