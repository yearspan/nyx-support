When you use the Services, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

1.3 Encrypted Communication Data

NYX Messenger supports end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for communications:

  • Messages are encrypted on your device before transmission.
  • We do not have access to the content of encrypted messages.
  • Only the intended recipients can decrypt and read the messages.
