Technical Support | NYX Messenger

If you experience any issues while using NYX Messenger, please contact our support team. We will make reasonable efforts to provide timely assistance, address reported issues, and continuously improve the user experience.

Support Services

Our support team can assist with questions or issues related to:

  • Account registration, login, and access.
  • App crashes, errors, or unexpected behavior.
  • Problems sending or receiving messages.
  • Reporting inappropriate content or user behavior.
  • General product questions, suggestions, and feedback.

How to Contact Support

If you need assistance, please contact us by email. To help us understand and investigate the issue, include a clear description of the problem and any relevant details in your message.

Information to Include

Where applicable, please provide the following information:

  • Your device model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • Your NYX Messenger app version.
  • A brief description of the issue and the steps that led to it.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if available.
  • The approximate date and time when the issue occurred.

Please do not include your password, verification codes, encryption keys, or other highly sensitive account information in your support email.

Response Time

We will make reasonable efforts to review and respond to support requests as soon as possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request and the volume of inquiries we receive.
